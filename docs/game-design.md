# Wizard-n-Which — Game Design

A side-scrolling adventure about witches and wizards, based on an idea by a 9-year-old game designer.

## Concept

Design your own witch or wizard — from the shape of the head all the way down to the shoes 👠 — then earn jewels 💎 by doing good deeds. Spend them in the shop on an animal helper to join you on your adventures 🧙🏻.

> Have a fun time, and be adventurous!

## Platform

- Browser game for **desktop and laptop** (keyboard controls).
- **Not supported on phones.** Show a friendly message on small touch devices.
- Modern, widely compatible web tech: plain HTML, CSS and ES modules; no build step.

## Game flow

1. **Open screen** — title and start button.
2. **Character choice** — pick witch or wizard.
3. **Character designer** — head shape, hair, clothes, hat, shoes, colours.
4. **Home** — the player starts as a kid living in their house with their parents.
5. **Adventure** — side-scrolling world; do good deeds to collect jewels.
6. **Shop** — spend jewels to buy an animal helper.
7. **Helper** — the animal follows the player on adventures.

## Art direction

Start with a very basic witch drawing (simple vector shapes, based on the daughter's drawing) and refine later.

### Notes from the original drawings

- **Character parts sheet:** pointy hats (starry and dark), capes and dresses for girl and boy variants, hair, face shapes, eyes, mouths and accessories — a good base for the character designer.
- **Animals:** owl, bat, black cat, spider.
- **Home scene:** a cosy house with a spiderweb, brooms, a cauldron, shelves of potions and a door.
- **Shop:** a building marked "Shop" with brooms and animals.
- **Witch sprite:** purple starry pointy hat, long straight purple robe.

## Milestones

Each milestone is split into small, separately committable features.

| # | Milestone | Features |
|---|-----------|----------|
| 1 | **Open screen** | Title screen, basic witch drawing, start button, phone notice |
| 2 | **Character choice** | Witch or wizard selection scene, scene manager |
| 3 | **Home scene** | Kid character in the house, side-scrolling movement with keyboard |
| 4 | **Character designer** | Head shape, then hair, clothes, hat, shoes and colours |
| 5 | **Good deeds & jewels** | NPCs needing help, deed interactions, jewel counter |
| 6 | **Shop & animal helper** | Shop scene, buying an animal, helper follows the player |
| 7 | **Adventure world** | Longer scrolling levels, more deeds, polish, sound |
